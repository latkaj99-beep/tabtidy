# Privacy Policy – TabTidy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

TabTidy is built to respect your privacy.

## What TabTidy accesses
- **Tab information** (titles, URLs, favicons, pinned/audio/group status) to display, filter, categorize, group, sleep and close your tabs.

## What TabTidy stores
All data is stored **only on your device** in `chrome.storage.local`:
- **Settings:** your auto-close options and domain whitelist.
- **Activity:** a last-used time for each open tab, removed when the tab closes.
- **Recently closed:** title, URL and time of up to 50 tabs TabTidy closed automatically.

## What TabTidy does NOT do
- ❌ No data is sent to any server. TabTidy makes no network requests.
- ❌ No analytics, tracking, ads or cookies.
- ❌ No reading of web page contents and no scripts injected into pages.
- ❌ No accounts or personal information collected.

## Deleting your data
Remove the extension at `chrome://extensions` and all of its stored data is deleted.
